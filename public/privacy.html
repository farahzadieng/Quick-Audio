Privacy Policy

Audio Quick Tools processes audio files locally in your browser. We do not operate an audio upload endpoint or send your media to a remote audio processing server.

What stays on your device?

Original files, decoded audio data, metadata, artwork, and converted output are processed locally on your device. Downloaded outputs are saved only when you choose to download them.

What gets stored?

The app uses your browser's local storage to keep favorite tag presets, appearance preference, and a limited history of action type and count. File contents and file names are not stored in this history. You may clear local browser storage at any time.

Network resources

The site downloads its application files from the hosting provider. The optional Libre Baskerville webfont is fetched from Google Fonts, which may receive usual HTTP request information. Conversion resources are hosted on the same origin and are cached after first use. We do not deliberately send audio data in these requests.

Offline access

Once required app components have been cached, core functionality may work offline. The browser can delete cached data or restrict background processing.

Hosting data

The web host may record standard access logs (IP address, request time, and URL) according to the hosting provider's policies. This policy cannot make promises on behalf of a future site owner regarding analytics or hosting retention; the operator must review this page before production deployment.

Contact and updates

The production site owner should add a reachable contact address and effective date before public launch.
